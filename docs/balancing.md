# Equipment ideas for later playtests

The table records the original proposals. The 0.2.0 build implements seven of them with the playtest rules in the [README](../README.md). Rootbound Longbow, Iron Pilgrim Boots, and Miser’s Quiver remain candidates.

| Candidate | Gift | Curse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duelist’s Fang | Consecutive hits on the same target gain 10% Power per hit, up to 50%. | First hit after switching targets deals 30% less damage. |
| Rootbound Longbow | Gain 40% range. | Lose 35% attack Power inside the inner half of original range. |
| Martyr’s Plate | Take 30% less damage. | Receive 50% less healing. |
| Gravedigger’s Lantern | Gain 40% Power against a target no ally is attacking. | Lose 15% Power against a shared target. |
| Iron Pilgrim Boots | After standing still for 4 seconds, take 30% less damage until moving. | Move 35% slower. |
| Hangman’s Quarrel | Deal 60% more damage below 20% target health. | Deal 15% less damage against other targets. |
| Emberwake Blade | Every fourth basic hit applies Burn. | Each trigger costs 5% of maximum health, down to 1 HP. |
| Leadheart Locket | Take 35% less damage below half health. | Healing caps at 75% maximum health. |
| Miser’s Quiver | Gain 30% range while the kingdom has at least 3 coins. | The first attack beyond normal range each night costs 1 coin. |
| The Borrowed Halo | Once per night, a lethal hit leaves the wearer at 1 HP with 2 seconds of invulnerability. | After triggering, lose attacks and abilities for 8 seconds. |
